# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 23 November 2026*

Gratis AI ("Gratis") runs AI models on your own device. Your conversations never leave it, unless you choose an optional Gratis Pro cloud model (see below).

## Who we are

Gratis is made by **Taofu B.V.**, Pedro de Medinalaan 83, 1086 XP Amsterdam, the Netherlands (KvK 94536872). Questions about privacy: [support@taofu.xyz](mailto:support@taofu.xyz).

## What we collect

**Nothing** in the free app. Gratis has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and on-device models need no account. Only if you sign in to Gratis Pro do your messages to a Pro model go to a server (see below).

## What stays on your device

- Your conversations, attachments and custom instructions are stored in the app's local storage on your device.
- AI models, once downloaded, are stored on your device and run there.
- Voice input is transcribed on your device by a local speech model.

You can delete everything at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser).

## Network connections Gratis makes

With on-device models, Gratis only goes online to fetch things, never to send your content:

| Connection | Why | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hugging Face (huggingface.co) | Downloading the AI models you choose and listing available models | A normal web request (your IP address, the file requested) |
| GitHub (github.com) | Desktop app only: checking for and downloading updates | A normal web request |
| gratis-ai.com (Cloudflare) | Web version only: loading the app itself and checking for app updates | A normal web request |
| account.gratis-ai.com | Gratis Pro only, after you sign in: your account, allowance and Pro model replies | See Gratis Pro below |

These services may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. Gratis sets no referrer when downloading models, and sends no identifiers of its own.

## Gratis Pro (optional, web version)

Gratis Pro is an optional paid add-on for faster, larger cloud models. The free, on-device app never needs it.

- You sign in with a Gratis account at account.gratis-ai.com. The app stores a sign-in token in your browser; signing out deletes it.
- When you pick a Pro model, the conversation you send to it (text only) goes to Gratis's servers to generate the reply. On-device models never send anything.
- Your chat history still lives only on your device.
- Your Gratis account and Pro usage are covered by the account service's own terms and privacy policy.

## Children

Gratis is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
