# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

Gratis AI ("Gratis") runs AI models on your own device. Your conversations never leave it.

## Who we are

Gratis is made by **Taofu B.V.**, Pedro de Medinalaan 83, 1086 XP Amsterdam, the Netherlands (KvK 94536872). Questions about privacy: [support@taofu.xyz](mailto:support@taofu.xyz).

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Gratis has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run a server that receives your messages, files, photos, voice or settings.

## What stays on your device

- Your conversations, attachments and custom instructions are stored in the app's local storage on your device.
- AI models, once downloaded, are stored on your device and run there.
- Voice input is transcribed on your device by a local speech model.

You can delete everything at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser).

## Network connections Gratis makes

Gratis only goes online to fetch things, never to send your content:

| Connection | Why | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hugging Face (huggingface.co) | Downloading the AI models you choose and listing available models | A normal web request (your IP address, the file requested) |
| GitHub (github.com) | Desktop app only: checking for and downloading updates | A normal web request |
| gratis.trueperformancenetwork.com (Cloudflare) | Web version only: loading the app itself and checking for app updates | A normal web request |

These services may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. Gratis sets no referrer when downloading models, and sends no identifiers of its own.

## Children

Gratis is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or complaint, email [support@taofu.xyz](mailto:support@taofu.xyz). You may also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
